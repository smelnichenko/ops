# Finland (FI) - check of the research

The research file has no section on the employer route; it is added in a later pass.

Checked on 2026-11-28. Client: curl 8.14.1 with its default User-Agent, one capped GET per URL, one at a time;
the Claude Code WebFetch tool for one page (Duunitori's terms: the site's robots.txt shuts out unnamed agents
such as curl and lets in "Claude-User", the fetch tool's agent; that tool relays text in fragments of at most
125 characters); web search for leads; the Internet Archive for one robots.txt. No account, key or login; no
call to `api.ahtp.fi`. At most three requests per host. Statistics Finland was not requested: out of purpose.

## Corrections and unverified claims (apply these first)

- CORRECTED: Kuntarekry's and Valtiolle.fi's pages both name the same operator: `<meta name="author"
  content="Grade Solutions Oy" />` (Kuntarekry also shows its logo). "FCG" and "Palkeet" appear on the pages only
  as employers in the lists. The "one platform" guess is thereby confirmed.
- CORRECTED: Duunitori's contracting party depends on the country: "Duunitori Oy Suomessa, JobbSafari AB Ruotsissa
  ja Jobbland AS Norjassa" - the same terms govern JobbSafari (Sweden) and Jobbland (Norway).
- CORRECTED: Duunitori's ban is fuller than the shortened quotation: "Käyttäjä ei saa tallentaa, arkistoida,
  skrapata tai muutoin järjestelmällisesti kopioida Palvelun sisältöä tai olennaista osaa siitä, paitsi siltä osin
  kuin se on teknisesti välttämätöntä Palvelun valtuutettua käyttöä varten." It binds all users, job seekers
  included.
- CORRECTED: Jobly's terms were found and read: Alma Media's "Markkinapaikkaehdot"
  (https://www.almamedia.fi/markkinapaikkaehdot/#jobly). They forbid robots, spiders and scrapers without written
  consent, and allow one copy for personal, non-commercial use.
- UNVERIFIED: everything taken from the technical page of the Job Market Finland interface - the endpoints
  `https://api.ahtp.fi/kipa/p67/v2/jobpostings` and `api-qa`, the header `KIPA-Subscription-Key`, NDJSON output,
  the code sets, and "the IP addresses openings are provided by the KEHA Center". Not requested: the three
  requests to tyomarkkinatori.fi went to the two terms pages and the description page, and the Internet Archive
  has no copy. A search summary today is consistent (key header, service P67, NDJSON) but is not the page.
- UNVERIFIED: the withdrawal of the old interfaces in May 2027 - not on the English pages read.
- UNVERIFIED: Valtiolle.fi's publisher (Palkeet for the Ministry of Finance) - not on the page read.
- UNVERIFIED: the redirects of `www.valtiolle.fi`, `valtiolle.fi/fi/`, `www.kuntarekry.fi`, `kuntarekry.fi/fi/` -
  not re-requested (request budget).
- UNVERIFIED: Oikotie Työpaikat's publisher and status - the host only redirects; a web search found no notice.
- UNVERIFIED: the Statistics Finland block (PxWeb, atp and tyonv tables, CC BY 4.0) - not requested: statistics.
- UNVERIFIED: third-party scrapers of duunitori.fi on apify.com - not searched today (one for Työmarkkinatori
  did appear in a search result).

## Found beyond the research

- Job Market Finland's site-wide Terms of Use (updated 5.11.2026) were read: no clause on automated reading or
  copying; the EURES sentence was added to them on 24.6.2025.
- Job Market Finland's robots.txt (archive copy of 2026-03-04) disallows `/api/` and `/*/api/` - the site's own
  interface behind the vacancy search - and leaves the vacancy pages open.
- Valtiolle.fi and Kuntarekry are run on one platform whose author tag is "Grade Solutions Oy"; adverts on it
  carry schema.org JobPosting data (seen on one Kuntarekry page).
- Duunitori's terms are group terms: they also bind users of JobbSafari (Sweden) and Jobbland (Norway).
- Jobly's terms are Alma Media's marketplace terms; they explicitly permit a personal copy and explicitly forbid
  robots and scrapers without written consent.
- The description page of the retrieval interface links a "FINESCO Skills and occupations" zip of code lists
  (`https://tyomarkkinatori.fi/dam/jcr:e9f1db95-5aad-4db4-bd7e-2d010479979a/definitions.zip`, not requested).
- Still unchecked, as in the research: LinkedIn, Indeed, Monster and sector boards in Finland; whether the State
  Treasury's RSS feed is a feed of valtiolle.fi.
