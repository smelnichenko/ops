# Regulation (EU) 2023/138: what it requires for company data

Read on 2026-11-28 from the EU Publications Office copy of the Official Journal text
(`http://publications.europa.eu/resource/celex/32023R0138`, asked for as `application/xhtml+xml` in
English; HTTP 200, 453,319 bytes; the page identifies itself as L 19/43, 20.1.2023, EN). This is the
text as published; a later amendment, if any, was not looked for.

## What the text says

- **Company data is one of the six categories.** Recital 4: "Annex I to Directive (EU) 2019/1024
  lays down the themes of high-value datasets by listing six thematic data categories: 1)
  geospatial; 2) earth observation and environment; 3) meteorological; 4) statistics; 5) companies
  and company ownership; and 6) mobility."
- **Datasets in scope (Annex, 5.1):** "datasets containing basic company information and company
  documents and accounts at individual company level". Key attributes of basic company information:
  - "Name of the company (full version; alternative names when applicable)"
  - "Company status (such as when it is closed, struck off the register, wound up, dissolved (as
    well as the date of these events), economically active or inactive as defined in national law)"
  - "Registration date"
  - "Registered office address"
  - "Legal form"
  - "Registration number"
  - "Member State where the company is registered"
  - "Activity/activities that are the object of the company, such as the NACE code"
- **Terms (Annex, 5.2):** the datasets "shall be made available for re-use" "without undue delay
  after the latest update"; "under the conditions of the Creative Commons BY 4.0 licence or any
  equivalent or less restrictive open licence, with additional conditions concerning the re-use of
  personal data where relevant"; in an open, machine-readable format; "through APIs and bulk
  download"; "at individual company level".
- **Date:** "This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its
  publication" and "shall apply from 16 months after entry into force". Published 20.1.2023, so in
  force 9 February 2023 and applying from 9 June 2024 (dates worked out from those two sentences).
- **Recital 10** encourages Member States "to go beyond the minimum requirements", "especially the
  category 'companies and company ownership'".

## What it does not say

- No website, e-mail address, telephone number or number of employees is among the key attributes.
  A register that meets the rule gives names, numbers, addresses, legal form, status and activity
  codes - not the address of an employer's careers page.
- It says what Member States must publish, not whether each one does. Per-country compliance is
  for the country files to establish.
