## Corrections and unverified claims (apply these first)

### Corrections (5)

1. **BG18 - Bulgaria, KAIS licence.** Notes: "none stated (unclear)", commercial reuse "legally unclear". Right value: no licence text on the page (true), but in the thread the notes cite, post 24 of 19 August 2025 reports AGKK's answer that the open data "може да се ползват за всякакви цели без ограничения", attribution to AGKK recommended, an "open licence" in the sense of Directive (EU) 2019/1024, and that AGKK will not add an explicit licence text. Secondary source (community report of AGKK's reply); the ordinance it cites was not read in an official text.
2. **BG24 - Bulgaria, GRAO address classifier.** Notes: "yearly datasets 2016-2025". Right value: nine editions in the EU catalogue - 2016, 2017, 2018, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025; no 2019 edition (and no 2026).
3. **CY9 - Cyprus, WMS URL.** Notes: `https://eservices.dls.moi.gov.cy/inspire/rest/services/INSPIRE/AD_Addresses/MapServer/WMSServer?...`. Right value: that URL (as printed on data.gov.cy) returns an HTML page; the working WMS is `https://eservices.dls.moi.gov.cy/inspire/services/INSPIRE/AD_Addresses/MapServer/WMSServer?request=GetCapabilities&service=WMS` (no `/rest`).
4. **RO21 - Romania, Esri Romania server.** Notes: "gave my client no response". Right value: `maps-gisportal.esriro.ro` resolves and answers, but with a self-signed TLS certificate, so a certificate-checking client cannot use it.
5. **X6 - HVD reporting duty.** Notes: "every two years starting February 2025", cited to a data.europa.eu news article. Right value: the article does not say this; Regulation (EU) 2023/138 Article 5 does - first report within 2 years of entry into force (February 2025), updates "upon the request of the Commission which should be made every 2 years", with persistent links to licensing conditions and APIs.

### Additions the report writer may want (not errors)

- Croatia: the Open Licence terms are now read in the official gazette (NN 67/2017, Prilog I), so they are no longer "secondary" and the "licence page unreadable" gap is closed (HR22, HR32). The "no charge" rule is in NN 39/22 Art. 1; NIPP source 1326 cited for it is the utility-cadastre service, not the address service (HR23). The Address.gml root says `numberReturned="10000"` with a `next` link (HR7). The "registered users" WMS answered GetCapabilities without credentials (HR17).
- Bulgaria: the 2020 project document sets the goal of ЦАИС "Адресен регистър" as "отворен и безплатен регистър" (BG14). Sofia GeoJSON has 125,245 addresses; the portal gives no CC BY version number (BG21). KAIS terms concern registered clients' services, not the open data (BG12).
- Greece: municipal address layers on data.gov.gr list CSV, GeoJSON, KML and SHAPE-ZIP downloads, not WMS only; more municipalities have them (GR23). The Eurostat quote describes the 2011 census (GR4). About 1% of NUM values in the first 400,000 records are 8+ digit strings (GR11). The embedded metadata also says "no limitations on public access" (GR15). The portal's "broken link" mark dates from 2026-02-03 (GR25).
- Cyprus: in the Cyprus Post CSV the street-name column labels EN/GR are swapped (CY13); 839 of the 857 range rows contain digits (CY14); identifiers and status sit in table 8, not layer 0 (CY7).
- Romania: HG 777/2016 Art. 1(3) says RENNS information "au caracter public" (RO3). 14 July 2026 is the day the systems went down; the intrusion began earlier (RO10). The ransom note claims RENNS source code, not data (RO12). The 2016 postcode file is 7,845,888 bytes (RO15).
- All: for Cyprus, Romania and Bulgaria no dataset of any HVD category is flagged in data.europa.eu (HR26, X3). `inspire-geoportal.ec.europa.eu` now aliases data.europa.eu and serves an expired TLS certificate (X4, X7).
