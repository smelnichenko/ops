## Research notes (first layer, as the researcher wrote them)

**Source section: Malta: which free address data sources exist, and on what terms?**

### Takeaway
Malta has no open address-point dataset. A statutory Address Register was created in September 2025 under a new Office of the Address Registrar, but what it publishes openly so far is reference lists only (6 regions, 85 localities, 68 local councils, 8,502 streets) through a CC BY 4.0 JSON API with no door numbers, coordinates or postcodes. Postcodes are held by MaltaPost, which offers a single-address lookup and, by regulatory decision, a full database on request at no cost.

### Cited Findings

**Address Register (Office of the Address Registrar)**
- The Office of the Address Registrar was set up under Legal Notice 205 of 2025 (Address Register and related Location (Geospatial) Registers Regulations, 2025), published 23 September 2025; it took over the Address Management Unit that had operated in the Electoral Office since 2016 (door numbering, street naming); the Address Register is intended as the authoritative registry of all streets and addresses in the Maltese archipelago, including spatial data — [address.gov.mt](https://address.gov.mt/)
- Public services on the site: "Research Spot" (search official door-numbering and street-naming publications from 1997), "Locate a Street", and registers of local councils, localities and regions; the page describes no address-level download — [address.gov.mt](https://address.gov.mt/)
- The API namespace exposes exactly these routes: `/gov/v1/regions`, `/gov/v1/localities`, `/gov/v1/local-councils`, `/gov/v1/streets`, `/gov/v1/openapi.json`; requests for `/addresses` or `/doors` return "rest_no_route" (verified 2026-11-11) — [address.gov.mt API index](https://address.gov.mt/wp-json/gov/v1)
- Verified content: streets endpoint returns 8,502 records (1.38 MB JSON) with `street_name`, `street_code`, `locality`, `region`, `local_council`; localities 85 records; regions 6; local councils 68; no coordinates, door numbers or postcodes in any of them; no key needed — [streets API](https://address.gov.mt/wp-json/gov/v1/streets); [localities API](https://address.gov.mt/wp-json/gov/v1/localities)
- Catalogue entries: "Streets Dataset", "Localities Dataset", "Regions Dataset" (issued 2026-03-27, modified 2026-09-14) and "Local Councils of Malta" (issued 2026-06-23) by the Office of the Address Registrar, each with one API distribution licensed CC BY 4.0; none is flagged as a high-value dataset — [data.europa.eu search, Malta "Address Registrar"](https://data.europa.eu/api/hub/search/search?q=Address%20Registrar&filter=dataset&facets=%7B%22country%22%3A%5B%22mt%22%5D%7D&limit=10)
- The Streets Dataset is defined as records of all publicly or privately owned streets that serve as thoroughfares and are assigned a name by the Registrar — [data.europa.eu record, Streets Dataset](https://data.europa.eu/api/hub/search/datasets/0d102ee6-a173-46b4-8b6d-bacf9bba93fb)

**Postcodes: MaltaPost**
- MaltaPost runs a web "Postcode Finder" (page title "MaltaPost - Postcode Finder", HTTP 200 on 2026-11-11); the older host `postcodes.maltapost.com` no longer resolves — [MaltaPost Postcode Finder](https://www.maltapost.com/PostCode/)
- The Malta Communications Authority decision of 14 May 2018 keeps the condition that "Third parties (in particular bulk mail senders) requiring a full postcode database will be provided with one at no cost", unless another entity makes an equivalent database available; MaltaPost stated it was already giving entities access to the whole postcode database and described itself as custodian and originator of Malta's postcode system — [MCA, Postcodes decision notice, 14 May 2018](https://www.mca.org.mt/sites/default/files/Postcodes%20Decision_1.pdf)
- A search of the EU data portal for Maltese datasets matching "postcode" returned zero results — [data.europa.eu search, Malta "postcode"](https://data.europa.eu/api/hub/search/search?q=postcode&filter=dataset&facets=%7B%22country%22%3A%5B%22mt%22%5D%7D&limit=10)

**Other bodies checked**
- Planning Authority: "Large Scale Topography Buildings" is flagged as a high-value dataset and served by WFS/WMS and OGC API under CC BY 4.0; it is a buildings dataset, not addresses — [Planning Authority buildings WFS](https://haleconnect.com/ows/services/org.1261.af1f626c-3200-4fb6-8ae0-a3426b6b4a6e_wfs?SERVICE=WFS&Request=GetCapabilities); [data.europa.eu search, Malta "buildings"](https://data.europa.eu/api/hub/search/search?q=buildings&filter=dataset&facets=%7B%22country%22%3A%5B%22mt%22%5D%7D&limit=6)
- INSPIRE Addresses theme: a search of Maltese datasets for "AD.Address" returned zero results, and "Addresses INSPIRE" returned only unrelated INSPIRE layers (orthoimagery, human health) — [data.europa.eu search, Malta "AD.Address"](https://data.europa.eu/api/hub/search/search?q=AD.Address&filter=dataset&facets=%7B%22country%22%3A%5B%22mt%22%5D%7D&limit=6)
- Identità lists "Person Basic Details" and "Person Household Details" (latest known address of persons) in the catalogue with no distributions, that is, not open — [data.europa.eu search, Malta "address"](https://data.europa.eu/api/hub/search/search?q=address&filter=dataset&facets=%7B%22country%22%3A%5B%22mt%22%5D%7D&limit=10)
- The national portals `portal.data.gov.mt` and `msdi.data.gov.mt` returned HTTP 403 with a Cloudflare challenge to non-browser clients on 2026-11-11, so their own catalogue pages could not be read directly — [portal.data.gov.mt, Streets Dataset](https://portal.data.gov.mt/dataset/streets-dataset)
- OpenAddresses has no source directory for Malta (GitHub API returns 404 for `sources/mt`) — [openaddresses/openaddresses sources](https://github.com/openaddresses/openaddresses/tree/master/sources)

### Inferences
- The best free official material for Malta is the street gazetteer API (useful for validating street names and their locality/council), which is not an address dataset. For house-number-level data with coordinates the realistic free option is OpenStreetMap, covered by another researcher.
- The 2025 legal basis and the stated aim that the register include spatial data make a future open address-point release plausible, but no date or commitment was found.
- Malta has not published addresses as a high-value dataset: the only address-related open datasets are not HVD-flagged, and the HVD-flagged geospatial item found is buildings.
- The MaltaPost full database "at no cost" is a request-based entitlement aimed at bulk mailers, not open data; reuse terms are unknown.

### Gaps
- Licence, format, content and procedure for obtaining MaltaPost's full postcode database: not published anywhere I could find.
- Whether the Address Register holds door-level points with coordinates that could be requested: not stated on address.gov.mt.
- The EU INSPIRE Geoportal could not be consulted (expired TLS certificate on 2026-11-11), so the absence of a Maltese INSPIRE Addresses dataset rests on the data.europa.eu search only.
- The text of Legal Notice 205 of 2025 (access and reuse provisions) was not read.
- Lands Authority: no address dataset found; its site was not examined in depth.
