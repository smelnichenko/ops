## Research notes (first layer, as the researcher wrote them)

**Source section: Italy: which free address data sources exist, and on what terms?**

### Takeaway
Italy's national register ANNCSU (Archivio Nazionale dei Numeri Civici delle Strade Urbane, run by ISTAT and the Agenzia delle Entrate) has been open since December 2024/February 2025: monthly national and regional CSV files under CC BY 4.0, no registration, with 27.4 million civic numbers in the 2 November 2026 file, 75.6% of them carrying coordinates. It has no postcodes (CAP remain a paid Poste Italiane product), and coordinate coverage is uneven between municipalities, so regional and municipal datasets remain useful complements.

### Cited Findings

**National register: ANNCSU (ISTAT + Agenzia delle Entrate)**
- ANNCSU is the national reference for municipal street directories; the site lists an "Open data" section, a street/civic-number search, an archive update status page and a georeferencing page; contacts are at ISTAT and the Agenzia delle Entrate — [anncsu.gov.it](https://www.anncsu.gov.it/)
- The open data page explains that Implementing Regulation (EU) 2023/138 placed addresses among geospatial high-value datasets that must be provided as open data, and that ISTAT and the Agenzia, with ANCI, adopted a new version of the ANNCSU technical specifications by inter-departmental provision of 12 December 2024 after a positive opinion of the data protection authority — [ANNCSU open data](https://www.anncsu.gov.it/it/consultazione-dellarchivio/open-data/)
- Datasets: street directories (stradari) and address lists (indirizzari) for the whole country or single regions, as CSV text files compressed as ZIP, "aggiornati mensilmente", with the creation date in the file name; data include "SNC" accesses (without a standard civic number) entered by municipalities in 2014–2016 — [ANNCSU open data](https://www.anncsu.gov.it/it/consultazione-dellarchivio/open-data/)
- Download links follow the pattern `https://anncsu.open.agenziaentrate.gov.it/age-inspire/opendata/anncsu/getds.php?INDIR_ITA` (national addresses), `?STRAD_ITA` (national streets) and regional codes such as `INDIR_LOMB`, `INDIR_VALL`, `INDIR_TREN` (20 regional pairs) — [ANNCSU bulk download page](https://www.anncsu.gov.it/it/consultazione-dellarchivio/open-data/Accedi-ai-servizi-di-dowload-massivo-in-Open-data/)
- Verified 2026-11-11: plain GET requests work without registration, key or referer (HEAD requests are answered with HTTP 403). National address file: `indirizzarioItalia.zip`, 336,334,007 bytes, containing `INDIR_ITA_20261102.csv` of 2,206,534,790 bytes; national street file `stradarioItalia.zip`, 13,920,082 bytes; Lombardia address file 36,906,989 bytes — [getds.php?INDIR_ITA](https://anncsu.open.agenziaentrate.gov.it/age-inspire/opendata/anncsu/getds.php?INDIR_ITA); [getds.php?STRAD_ITA](https://anncsu.open.agenziaentrate.gov.it/age-inspire/opendata/anncsu/getds.php?STRAD_ITA)
- Own count in `INDIR_ITA_20261102.csv`: 27,413,752 address rows; 20,728,959 (75.62%) have coordinates; 818,455 rows are flagged SNC; 7,891 distinct municipality codes appear, 5,502 of them with at least one coordinate and 4,454 with coordinates on every row — [getds.php?INDIR_ITA](https://anncsu.open.agenziaentrate.gov.it/age-inspire/opendata/anncsu/getds.php?INDIR_ITA)
- Own count of coordinate method codes: 10,892,898 rows method 4, 7,118,388 method 3, 1,721,955 method 1, 705,060 method 5, 290,658 method 2 — [getds.php?INDIR_ITA](https://anncsu.open.agenziaentrate.gov.it/age-inspire/opendata/anncsu/getds.php?INDIR_ITA)
- Coverage is uneven: in the Valle d'Aosta file (94,256 rows, 74 municipalities) only 20.4% of rows and 20 municipalities have coordinates — [getds.php?INDIR_VALL](https://anncsu.open.agenziaentrate.gov.it/age-inspire/opendata/anncsu/getds.php?INDIR_VALL)
- Fields of the address file (semicolon-separated, UTF-8, decimal comma): `CODICE_COMUNE` (Belfiore code), `CODICE_ISTAT`, `PROGRESSIVO_NAZIONALE` (national street id), `CODICE_COMUNALE`, `ODONIMO`, `LOCALITA'`, `DIZIONE_LINGUA1`, `DIZIONE_LINGUA2` (bilingual names), `PROGRESSIVO_ACCESSO` (national access id), `CODICE_COMUNALE_ACCESSO`, `CIVICO`, `ESPONENTE`, `SPECIFICITA` (for example ROSSO/NERO), `METRICO`, `PROGRESSIVO_SNC`, `COORD_X_COMUNE`, `COORD_Y_COMUNE`, `QUOTA`, `METODO`. There is no postcode (CAP) field — [metadata_indirizzario.json](https://www.anncsu.gov.it/.allegati/metadata_indirizzario.json)
- Coordinates are longitude/latitude in decimal degrees (7 decimals) in the national geodetic reference system ETRF2000 epoch 2008.0 (ETRS89 realisation); `METODO` codes: 1 field survey with accuracy under 5 m, 2 field survey ≥ 5 m, 3 indirect derivation from a territorial database under 5 m, 4 indirect derivation ≥ 5 m, 5 derived through the functions of the municipalities' portal — [metadata_indirizzario.json](https://www.anncsu.gov.it/.allegati/metadata_indirizzario.json)
- Street file fields include `TOTALE_ACCESSI` (number of civic numbers per street) — [metadata_stradario.json](https://www.anncsu.gov.it/.allegati/metadata_stradario.json)
- Coordinates exist only where the municipality entered them; each access has a numeric code that never changes even if the civic number or street name changes; coordinates are not historised; the site reported 4,304 municipalities with all civic numbers georeferenced at 31/03/2026 — [ANNCSU georeferencing page](https://www.anncsu.gov.it/it/consultazione-dellarchivio/georeferenziazione-numeri-civici/); [anncsu.gov.it](https://www.anncsu.gov.it/)
- Licence and HVD status: the catalogue entry "Stradari ed indirizzari regionali e nazionali da ANNCSU" (publisher Agenzia delle Entrate, 42 distributions) is licensed CC-BY-4.0, flagged as a high-value dataset (category Geospatial), cites Implementing Regulation 2023/138 as applicable legislation, has monthly periodicity and a modification date of 2024-12-10 — [data.europa.eu record (API)](https://data.europa.eu/api/hub/search/datasets/age-strad_ind_italia~~1)
- Press coverage of 26/02/2025 announced the Agenzia delle Entrate open data services as active: street and address lists updated monthly, point services updated daily, licence CC BY 4.0 with citation of the source; the same release opened cadastral parcels by WFS and bulk GML (Trento and Bolzano excluded from the cadastral bulk download) — [LavoriPubblici, 26/02/2025](https://www.lavoripubblici.it/news/stampa/35094)
- Query API: `querydata.php` returns JSON for street names (`resource=odonimi&codicecomune=H501&denominazione=...`) and civic numbers (`resource=accessi&progressivoodonimo=...&accesso=...`); it answered without a key on 2026-11-11 — [ANNCSU querydata service](https://anncsu.open.agenziaentrate.gov.it/age-inspire/opendata/anncsu/querydata.php)
- Earlier state for contrast (secondary, search summaries not opened in full): a 2022 OSM mailing-list post expected bulk download without coordinates, and a 2025 presentation reported 4.9% of records with coordinates — [talk-it, May 2022](https://lists.openstreetmap.org/pipermail/talk-it/2022-May/073872.html); [ANNCSU presentation, July 2025](https://www.sportellotelematicopolifunzionale.it/sites/default/files/2025-07/Presentazione%20ANCSSU%20.pdf)
- OpenAddresses now uses ANNCSU as its Italy countrywide source (CC-BY 4.0, attribution "Istat e dall'Agenzia delle Entrate"; cached file `INDIR_ITA_20260703_GEO.csv`) — [OpenAddresses sources/it/countrywide.json](https://github.com/openaddresses/openaddresses/blob/master/sources/it/countrywide.json)

**Postcodes: Poste Italiane**
- Poste Italiane FAQ (June 2019): complete CAP files cannot be downloaded for free because "la banca dati del CAP è di proprietà di Poste Italiane che ne detiene i diritti intellettuali ed industriali"; only lookup tools (Cerca CAP, contact centre) are free; CAP Professional (MS Access) and CAP Zone (shapefile) are commercial products — [Poste Italiane, FAQ banche dati CAP](https://business.poste.it/business/files/1473808238165/banchedati-faq-cap.pdf)

### Inferences
- ANNCSU is now the primary free source for Italy and makes most older regional extracts secondary. Regional and municipal files still matter for (a) the roughly one quarter of civic numbers without coordinates, (b) better positional accuracy (over half of ANNCSU coordinates are method 4, estimated accuracy of 5 m or worse), and (c) postcodes, which a few municipal datasets such as Bologna include.
- The count of 7,891 municipality codes suggests almost every Italian municipality is present in the file, but presence does not prove that each municipality's civic numbers are complete; this was not tested.
- The jump from a reported 4.9% georeferenced (2025, secondary) to 75.6% (November 2026, own count) implies a large loading campaign during 2025–2026; method codes 3–5 dominate, so most new coordinates were derived from existing databases, not surveyed.
- A full Italian address with postcode cannot be built from open official data alone: CAP must come from Poste Italiane (paid) or non-official sources.
- Parsing notes: coordinates use a decimal comma, the delimiter is a semicolon, and X is longitude.

### Gaps
- Official list of municipalities whose ANNCSU data are incomplete or unvalidated: the status page loads its figures by script and could not be read.
- No official statement of rate limits or terms for `querydata.php` was found.
- No region-wide open address dataset was found for Veneto or the Province of Trento in the searches run (only municipal files such as Verona and Venezia).
- Lombardia, Umbria and Piemonte regional downloads could not be confirmed live on 2026-11-11.
- ISTAT publishes no separate address download that I could find; its role is co-ownership of ANNCSU.
