## Research notes (first layer, as the researcher wrote them)

**Source section: 1. Germany, national level: is there a free nationwide address dataset (BKG / AdV / ZSHH)?**

### Takeaway
No. The two nationwide products are still closed: BKG's "Georeferenzierte Adressdaten (GA)" is only for federal authorities, and the ZSHH's "Hauskoordinaten Deutschland (HK-DE)" is sold (flat EUR 6,000 net per nationwide delivery under the AdV fee directive 4.0.1 in force since 9 June 2024). Free official address data exists only Land by Land (section 2); no official merged open download was found.

### Cited Findings
- BKG "Georeferenzierte Adressdaten (GA)": "Dieses Produkt steht nur einem eingeschränkten Kreis Nutzungsberechtigter zur Verfügung"; entitled users are "Bundesbehörden und Nutzungsberechtigte nach V GeoBund"; price shown as 0,00 EUR for them only; CSV (semicolon), one line per address, UTM32; "Fortführungszyklus: halbjährlich"; data state 10.2025; "Die Daten sind urheberrechtlich geschützt" — [BKG GA product page](https://gdz.bkg.bund.de/index.php/default/georeferenzierte-adressdaten-ga.html)
- HK-DE is bundled by the ZSHH (at the Bavarian LDBV), which "stellt Kunden diese im Auftrag der Länder zu einheitlichen Gebühren und Lizenzen ... zentral bereit"; HK-DE = "exakte Lagekoordinaten (Format .txt) für rund 23 Mio. adressierte Gebäude" — [LDBV ZSHH page](https://www.ldbv.bayern.de/vermessung/zshh/); same wording ("zu einheitlichen Gebühren- und Lizenzmodellen") — [AdV HK-DE page](https://www.adv-online.de/de/produkte/hauskoordinaten-deutschland-hk-de)
- HK-DE price: AdV Fee Directive version 4.0.1 of 27 April 2024, Table B.2 "Flat amounts for ALKIS products": House coordinates EUR 6,000 (Building polygons 4,000; LoD2 16,000). "The flat rate for the spatially unlimited provision ... is incurred again with every provision, but only once a year." For geocoding services the provision fee applies; for viewing and location search services "a flat rate of 5% of the fee ... is charged annually" — [AdV-GR 4.0.1 (English)](https://sg.geodatenzentrum.de/web_public/gdz/lizenz/eng/adv_gbr_eng.pdf)
- Official 2024 press release of the three central offices: nationwide Hauskoordinaten etc. "sind ab sofort zu deutlich günstigeren Konditionen erhältlich. Basis dafür ist die neue AdV-Gebührenrichtlinie 4.0, die bereits seit dem 9. Juni 2024 in Kraft ist"; provision "nach Maßgabe der Regelungen im Datennutzungsgesetz (DNG) vom 16. Juli 2021"; and "Für ihr jeweiliges Gebiet sind die originären Daten in den Ländern erhältlich. Dabei handelt es sich überwiegend um hochwertige Datensätze, die entgeltfrei genutzt werden dürfen." The central offices' own open data list contains only DTK100 and basemap.de services, no addresses — [Press release PDF (file dated 1 July 2024)](https://www.laiv-mv.de/static/LAIV/Geoinformation/Dateien/Blickpunkte/PM-Daten_der_Zentralen_Stellen_OD.pdf)
- HK-DE size, release 04-2026 (document dated 17.06.2026): 23,126,517 records in total (quality A 22,130,203; B 652,505; C 343,809); per Land: SH 952,280; HH 284,547; NI 2,635,374; HB 176,304; NW 4,502,736; HE 1,630,802; RP 1,406,086; BW 3,377,305; BY 3,767,086; SL 335,348; BE 394,933; BB 867,290; MV 518,416; SN 988,867; ST 664,183; TH 624,960 — [HK-DE info 04-2026](https://sg.geodatenzentrum.de/public/gdz/dokumentation/deu/hk-de_info.pdf)
- Länder deliver complete datasets to the ZSHH on 01.04. and 01.10. each year (per search-engine summary of the HK-DE format description; not opened by me) — [HK-DE format description v5.2](https://ldbv.bayern.de/mam/ldbv/dateien/datenformatbeschreibung_hk-de_v_5.2.pdf)
- BKG/AdV geocoding service ("Geokodierungsdienst der AdV für Adressen und Geonamen", valid from product state 2024-04): free ("geldleistungsfrei") for federal authorities under V GeoBund and for Länder authorities under V GeoLänder; for third parties "Die Lizenzierung erfolgt gegen Entgelt über die Zentrale Stelle Geotopographie (ZSGT) ... gemäß der jeweils gültigen AdV-Gebührenrichtlinie" — [Terms of use, AdV geocoding service](https://sg.geodatenzentrum.de/public/gdz/lizenz/deu/nutzungsbedingungen_geokodierungsdienst_adv.pdf)
- When federal bodies pass on applications containing Hauskoordinaten, the licensee "darf ... postalische Adressdaten nicht extrahieren und in bearbeiteter oder unbearbeiteter Form weitergeben oder öffentlich zugänglich machen" (document state 15.11.2024) — [Nutzungsbedingungen Hauskoordinaten V GeoBund 2024](https://sg.geodatenzentrum.de/web_public/gdz/lizenz/deu/nutzungsbedingungen_hk-de.pdf)
- BKG "Postleitzahlgebiete Deutschland (PLZ)" (valid from product state 2026-02, document 21.07.2026): provided free only to federal authorities / V GeoBund users; source credit "© Deutsche Post Direkt GmbH" — [BKG PLZ terms](https://sg.geodatenzentrum.de/public/gdz/lizenz/deu/nutzungsbedingungen_plz.pdf)
- INSPIRE address services exist per Land, not nationally. GovData lists INSPIRE address datasets for SN, SH, NW, HE, RP, SL, BE, HH, TH and BB with open licences (dl-de/by-2-0, dl-de/zero-2-0 or CC BY 4.0) — [GovData search "INSPIRE Adressen"](https://www.govdata.de/ckan/api/3/action/package_search?q=title:INSPIRE%20AND%20title:Adressen)
- HVD tagging (applicable legislation = Implementing Regulation (EU) 2023/138) is present on the GovData records of Brandenburg "Georeferenzierte Adresse", Baden-Württemberg "ALKIS-Hauskoordinaten", Sachsen-Anhalt "Gebäudereferenzen", Rheinland-Pfalz "Hauskoordinaten" and Berlin "Adressen Berlin" — [BB record](https://www.govdata.de/suche/daten/georeferenzierte-adresse-land-brandenburgdf59f), [BW record](https://www.govdata.de/suche/daten/alkis-hauskoordinaten), [ST record](https://www.govdata.de/suche/daten/gebaudereferenzen-sachsen-anhalt), [RP record](https://www.govdata.de/suche/daten/hauskoordinaten), [BE record](https://www.govdata.de/suche/daten/adressen-berlin)
- 2026 court development: netzpolitik.org reports that the OLG München dismissed Bavaria's civil suit against an open-data activist as inadmissible on 30 July 2026 (the dispute began in 2021 after nationwide house-coordinate data from the ZSHH was mirrored on GitHub and taken down); the court held the Datennutzungsgesetz applies; the activist says "Es gibt noch keine Rechtssicherheit für OpenData"; the judgment was unpublished when the article appeared — [netzpolitik.org 2026](https://netzpolitik.org/2026/mit-urheberrecht-gegen-offene-daten-bayern-verliert-gegen-open-data-aktivisten/)

### Inferences
- A Germany address file can today be assembled for free from 15 Länder (see section 2; Niedersachsen only by deriving addresses from open ALKIS, and three Länder's downloads did not work for me); Bavaria (3.77 million of 23.13 million HK-DE records, about 16%) is the gap. The official way to get a complete national file is HK-DE at EUR 6,000 net per yearly delivery.
- Under AdV-GR 4.0.1 a location-search (non-persistent) service on house coordinates would cost 5% of EUR 6,000 = EUR 300 per year; this is my arithmetic from the directive's rule, not a published price.
- The HVD regulation did not open the nationwide products; it shows up as HVD tags on individual Länder datasets.

### Gaps
- I could not read the full list of the 106 products on BKG's open-data page (only the first 10 rendered; none was an address product), so "BKG has no open address product" rests on the GA and PLZ terms above, not on a full list — [BKG open data page](https://gdz.bkg.bund.de/index.php/default/open-data.html)
- The current ZSHH terms (AGNB 5.0 of 9 June 2024) were not read in full — [AGNB ZSHH 5.0](https://ldbv.bayern.de/mam/ldbv/dateien/2024-06-09_agnb_zshh_5_0.pdf)
- No official statement was found on whether Germany reports the nationwide HK-DE as HVD-compliant; the national HVD compliance report was not located.

**Source section: 2. Germany, per Land: are Hauskoordinaten / ALKIS address data open? (table of all 16 Länder)**

### Takeaway
14 of 16 Länder offer a free house-number-level address-point product with coordinates; Niedersachsen offers none but its open ALKIS data contains the house numbers; Bavaria offers nothing free (only building outlines are open). I downloaded and inspected the files of 10 Länder (BW, BE, HB, HH, HE, MV, NW, RP, SN, ST); three listed-as-open downloads could not be fetched on 2026-11-11 (Saarland: password page; Thüringen: outage page and CAPTCHA; Schleswig-Holstein: broken TLS chain), and Brandenburg has no static file link. The big caveat is the postcode: BW, HE, MV, NW, RP, ST and Berlin's HK file are published "without postal data".

#### Cross-cutting facts
- The common file layout is the AdV "HK-DE" text format; header as shipped by BW: nba;oid;qua;landschl;land;regbezschl;regbez;kreisschl;kreis;gmdschl;gmd;ottschl;ott;strschl;str;hnr;adz;zone;ostwert;nordwert;postplz;postonm;postonmzus;postott;poststr;aud — [hk_bw.zip](https://opengeodata.lgl-bw.de/data/hk/hk_bw.zip)

### Inferences
- Postcodes are the weak point of the German open data: verified present only in Sachsen, Hamburg, Bremen and the Berlin WFS; described as present in Brandenburg; absent in BW, HE, MV, NW, RP, ST and Berlin's HK file. The pattern (and BW's wording "Informationen der Post") suggests the postal fields come from Deutsche Post data that the Länder do not release as open data; that is my reading, not a quoted statement.
- Licences split three ways: dl-de/zero-2-0 with no attribution (BE, HE, NW), dl-de/by-2-0 (BW, BB, HH, RP, SL, SN, ST) and CC BY 4.0 (HB, NI, SH, TH; BY for its other open data). All three permit commercial reuse; none is share-alike.
- No free download I tested needed a key or login, except Saarland's share link (password page). Hessen's link works without login but changes daily.
- Dates in the "Open since" column that come from catalogue "issued" fields are metadata dates and may be later than the real opening.

### Gaps
- Schleswig-Holstein: download page and file not opened (the server's TLS certificate chain could not be verified; I did not bypass verification). Update cycle, exact file URL and postcode content are unverified.
- Thüringen: the official file link returned an outage notice and the portal is behind a CAPTCHA; file content, cycle and postcode content are unverified. It may be a temporary outage or bot blocking.
- Saarland: the published download link leads to a password prompt; whether a password is published elsewhere or the file is reachable interactively is unverified.

- Niedersachsen, Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, NRW: prices and terms of the full Hauskoordinaten products with postal data were not found.
- Opening dates for BW, BB, HE, MV, NI, RP, SN were not found on official pages.
- Update cycles for HE and RP were not found on official pages.

**Source section: 3. Germany, other sources: Deutsche Post, free postcode data, statistical office, community-merged datasets**

### Takeaway
Deutsche Post's street and postcode data (Datafactory) is a paid product and BKG's postcode-area product is restricted to federal users; free street-level postcode data comes from community projects (OpenPLZ API, OpenStreetMap). OpenAddresses is the only merged collection of the Länder files found, with Bavaria limited to building outlines.

### Cited Findings
- Deutsche Post Direkt "Datafactory" is a commercial product line; the street directory is based on the original postal routing data with over 1.2 million street names and quarterly updates (search-engine summary of the product page; no price list found) — [Deutsche Post Direkt Datafactory](https://www.deutschepost.de/de/d/deutsche-post-direkt/datafactory.html)
- BKG's "Postleitzahlgebiete Deutschland" carries the credit "© Deutsche Post Direkt GmbH" and is provided only to federal authorities and V GeoBund users — [BKG PLZ terms, 21.07.2026](https://sg.geodatenzentrum.de/public/gdz/lizenz/deu/nutzungsbedingungen_plz.pdf)
- OpenPLZ API: "ein öffentliches Strassen- und Postleitzahlverzeichnis für Deutschland, Liechtenstein, die Schweiz und Österreich", REST API at https://openplzapi.org/ (for example /de/Streets, /de/Localities, /de/FullTextSearch); street names, postcodes, localities and municipalities; no house numbers; examples send no API key — [OpenPLZ API](https://www.openplzapi.org/de/)
- OpenAddresses holds source definitions for all 16 Länder; Bavaria has only building-outline sources (7 Regierungsbezirke); five Länder (BB, MV, NI, SH, SL) are served from files cached on OpenAddresses' own storage in August/September 2026 instead of a live official URL — [OpenAddresses sources/de](https://github.com/openaddresses/openaddresses/tree/master/sources/de)
- Municipal open data fills some gaps with full addresses, for example Frankfurt am Main Hauskoordinaten (CSV, dl-de/by-2-0), Rostock addresses (CSV, CC0 1.0, file Last-Modified 10 Nov 2026), Köln and Wesel addresses (dl-de/zero-2-0) — [OA Frankfurt](https://github.com/openaddresses/openaddresses/blob/master/sources/de/he/city_of_frankfurtammain.json), [Rostock file](https://geo.sv.rostock.de/download/opendata/adressen/adressen.csv), [OA Rostock](https://github.com/openaddresses/openaddresses/blob/master/sources/de/mv/city_of_rostock.json), [OA Köln](https://github.com/openaddresses/openaddresses/blob/master/sources/de/nw/city_of_cologne.json)
- Essen, Hagen and Bielefeld publish their own Hauskoordinaten on GovData (Essen tagged HVD) — [GovData search "Hauskoordinaten"](https://www.govdata.de/ckan/api/3/action/package_search?q=title:Hauskoordinaten)
- A nationwide house-coordinate copy mirrored on GitHub in 2021 was removed after a takedown demand by the Bavarian LDBV — [netzpolitik.org 2021](https://netzpolitik.org/2021/offene-geodaten-bayern-geht-gegen-open-data-aktivistinnen-vor/)

### Inferences
- For a Germany file with postcodes, the open Länder files must be joined with a separate postcode source (for example OpenStreetMap postcode areas) in the Länder that omit postal fields.
- OpenAddresses' reliance on cached uploads for five Länder confirms that those sources are not reliably machine-downloadable.

### Gaps
- No Deutsche Post price list was found; "paid" rests on the product being sold through Deutsche Post Direkt.
- OpenPLZ API's data sources, licence and rate limits were not on the page I read.
- Statistical-office sources (for example the Destatis municipality directory with postcodes) were not researched; I found no house-number-level statistical source.
- A QGIS plugin that claims to download the open data of all 16 Länder appeared in search results but was not checked.

**Source section: 5. Both countries: free APIs, licences and commercial reuse, caveats, 2024-2026 changes, HVD status**

### Takeaway
Bulk files are the dependable free route in both countries; free feature services (WFS, OGC API Features, ATOM) exist in most German Länder and at BEV, none of those I probed asked for a key, and none publishes rate limits. All licences found (dl-de/zero-2-0, dl-de/by-2-0, CC BY 4.0) allow commercial reuse; the practical obstacles are missing postcodes, unstable links and Bavaria.

### Inferences
- Commercial reuse is permitted everywhere data is free; the only obligations are attribution (dl-de/by-2-0, CC BY 4.0, BEV's dated credit) and, for Tirol's regional data, a notification.
- For an automated pipeline the fragile German sources are Hessen (daily-changing path), Hamburg (dated file names), Saarland, Thüringen, Schleswig-Holstein and Brandenburg (no stable anonymous file URL confirmed).
- Bavaria is the one clear case where address data of an HVD theme is not free; whether that complies with the regulation is contested and was not decided by the 2026 ruling as reported.

### Gaps
- No rate limits or fair-use rules were found for any of the services; none is documented on the pages I read.
- Services were tested only with capability or small requests; bulk extraction limits (maximum features per request) were not tested.
- The text of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2023/138 was not opened in this research; the statement that addresses fall under its geospatial theme rests on the HVD tags the publishers themselves set.
- NRW's INSPIRE address WFS URL I guessed returned 404; the correct endpoint is in the GovData record "INSPIRE NW Adressen ALKIS" and was not probed.
